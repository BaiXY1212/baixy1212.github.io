<p class="project-detail__lead">This project explores how structural equation modelling can help examine relationships within digital heritage experiences—for example, how access, interpretation, usability and perceived value may relate to engagement.</p>

<div class="project-detail__facts">
  <div><span>Domain</span>Digital heritage</div>
  <div><span>Method</span>Structural equation modelling</div>
  <div><span>Stage</span>Conceptual model development</div>
</div>

## What the method can contribute

Structural equation modelling offers a way to examine several related constructs together, including indirect relationships that are difficult to understand through isolated measures.

The value of the method depends on careful conceptual definition, appropriate data and transparent reporting. The model should support the research question—not replace it.

## Current next step

Define the constructs and plausible relationships clearly before deciding how they should be measured or tested.
