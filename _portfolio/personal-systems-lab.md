<p class="project-detail__lead">Personal Systems Lab is an experiment in supporting sustainable progress without turning everyday life into an endless productivity contest.</p>

<div class="project-detail__facts">
  <div><span>Format</span>Browser-based tool</div>
  <div><span>Data</span>Stored on your device</div>
  <div><span>Stage</span>Working prototype</div>
</div>

## What it currently includes

- Daily check-ins and time blocks
- Focus tasks and habits
- Short reflection prompts
- Personal trends and lightweight achievements
- Manual JSON backup and restore

## Privacy model

The public page contains the tool, but your entries remain in the browser storage of the device you use. No GitHub token is requested, and the tool does not automatically upload personal records.
