<p class="project-detail__lead">This research direction asks a practical question: how might museums design experiences with blind and low-vision visitors, rather than adding isolated accessibility features after the main experience has already been defined?</p>

<div class="project-detail__facts">
  <div><span>Focus</span>Inclusive cultural experience</div>
  <div><span>Perspective</span>Visitor-centred design</div>
  <div><span>Stage</span>Developing research scope</div>
</div>

## Questions guiding the work

- Where do barriers appear before, during and after a museum visit?
- What makes interpretation useful, engaging and self-directed?
- How can visitor expertise shape both research and design decisions?

## Working principle

Accessibility is not a checklist attached to an otherwise finished experience. It influences how information is structured, how choice is offered, how confidence is built and how visitors participate.
