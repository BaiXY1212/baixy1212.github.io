“Engagement” is often measured through visible activity alone. This project explores a more useful question: what makes an experience meaningful to the person taking part?

FocusMeaningful participation
OutputConceptual framework
StageRefining dimensions

Working dimensions

The framework is being developed around qualities such as agency, relevance, connection, comprehension and continuity. The aim is not to create a universal score, but to make assumptions about “good engagement” easier to discuss and test.

Why it matters

A clearer framework can connect research evidence with practical design decisions. It can also help distinguish short-term attention from an experience that supports understanding, belonging or future action.

This is an evolving research artefact. Definitions and relationships will be updated as the work develops.
