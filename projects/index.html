SELECTED WORK

Research & Projects

Questions I am investigating, work I have completed, and small systems I am building along the way.

My work sits between data science and the human experience of technology. I am especially interested in cultural spaces: how people encounter them, who is excluded, and how evidence and interaction design might create more meaningful forms of participation.

ACCESSIBILITY · CULTURAL EXPERIENCE

Museum experiences beyond the visual

Research direction

I am exploring how museums could support richer participation for blind and low-vision visitors through sound, touch, spatial guidance, and social interaction.

Current focus: understanding where access becomes passive accommodation, and where multisensory design can support agency, interpretation, and connection.

HCI · CONCEPT DEVELOPMENT

What makes engagement meaningful?

Developing

Interactive technology can attract attention without creating a valuable experience. This direction asks how participation, emotion, sensory experience, and reflection might be described and evaluated together.

Next step: turn broad ideas into a small literature map and a testable set of research questions before committing to a larger project.
DATA · DIGITAL HERITAGE

Evidence for cultural questions

Completed foundation

My undergraduate thesis examined the factors influencing digital legacy development using PESTEL analysis and structural equation modelling, supported by 504 survey responses.

What it shaped: an interest in combining quantitative evidence with cultural and social questions, rather than treating data as separate from lived experience.
