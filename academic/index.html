🎯 Research Interests#

My research interests lie at the intersection of Human–Computer Interaction (HCI) and Data Science, with a focus on:

  1. Accessibility & Multisensory Interaction
    How sound, touch, and spatial cues can supplement or reinterpret visual information—especially in cultural and museum settings.

  2. Digital Culture & Data Experience
    How people understand, preserve, and emotionally relate to their digital traces (built on my undergraduate research on digital legacy).

  3. Narrative Visualization & Spatial Storytelling
    Designing visual and interactive systems that shape interpretation, emotion, and engagement.

  4. Human-Centered AI
    Interpretable, culturally-aware AI systems that support meaningful human experiences.

💻 Research Projects#

1. Digital Legacy Attitudes Among Chinese Internet Users#

Undergraduate Thesis — Awarded Provincial Outstanding Thesis

  • Framework: Developed a hybrid method combining PESTEL qualitative analysis with SEM quantitative modelling.
  • Analysis: Collected 504 valid survey responses and identified six key sociocultural and psychological factors shaping digital heritage attitudes.
  • Outcome: Revealed ethical, emotional, and cultural implications of digital legacy, contributing to understanding how individuals conceptualize posthumous digital identity.

2. Tourism Destination Perception via Text Mining#

Course Project

  • Method: Built a custom tokenizer, Word2Vec embeddings, and fuzzy evaluation models to analyze 84,000+ Chinese travel reviews.
  • Analysis: Conducted sentiment scoring, clustering, and perception mapping.
  • Outcome: Produced interpretable destination–feature matrices validated through MSE-based metrics and expert review.

3. Public Opinion & E-Commerce Text Mining#

National Innovation Competition — 2nd Prize

  • Pipeline: Designed topic modelling and sentiment monitoring pipelines for large-scale multi-source datasets.
  • Tech: Implemented TF-IDF, topic classification, keyword extraction, and linguistic deception analysis.
  • Outcome: Delivered dashboards supporting strategic decision-making for business trend assessment.
